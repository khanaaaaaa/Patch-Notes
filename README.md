# Patch Notes

## What It Is

A visual novel built in Ren'Py about Eli, a girl navigating the slow unraveling of a world she didn't know was constructed around her.

## Why I Made It

I've always been drawn to stories about the specific kind of harm that comes from people who cause damage just being desperate to just survuve (aka villainess manhwa stories). I also wanted to write something that felt more like reading a novel than playing a game. I wanted to see if a visual novel could carry that weight while still giving the player real choices that matter.

## How It Was Made

**Tech stack:** Ren'Py

The script is split across multiple files by chapter. The three endings are tied to how Eli processes what she learns: whether she forgives, stays uncertain, or walks away. 

## What I Learnt and What I Struggled With

The hardest part in Ren'Py is keeping track of what variables are set where. The second hardest part was keeping the tone consistent. 

On the technical side, getting backgrounds to scale properly across different image formats (jpg, avif, webp) took longer than expected due to an error in how `bg_fit` was initially defined. UTF-8 encoding issues with PowerShell's `Set-Content` also caused corrupted em dashes in the script files.

## AI Usage

Used AI for debugging the notebook on screen, was following a tutorial but struggles a lot with it.
